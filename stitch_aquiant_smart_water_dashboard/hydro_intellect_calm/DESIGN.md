## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an authoritative, deeply calm, and intelligent interface for enterprise water infrastructure, municipal conservation networks, and commercial climate-tech operations. It translates real-time telemetry, hydrodynamic modeling, and consumption anomalies into a state of quiet precision. The interface must never feel alarmed or chaotic; it projects the measured clarity of deep oceanic layers and analytical composure.

The visual direction merges **Minimalist Modernism** with **Atmospheric Precision Glass**:
- **Tranquil Authority:** Deep midnight substrates evoke depth and vastness without visual fatigue, allowing critical telemetry to emerge cleanly.
- **Architectural Breathing Space:** Expansive padding and deliberate whitespace frame vital sensor matrices and hydraulic models, treating environmental data with high-end editorial gravity.
- **Zero-Neon Discipline:** Accent colors are applied strictly to intentional focus targets, micro-metrics, and actionable triggers—eradicating generic dashboard visual noise in favor of pristine situational clarity.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-refined dark atmosphere anchored in stratified oceanic slates, punctuated by luminous hydro-electric accents.

### Core Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#0B111E`):** Deep oceanic void; grounding substrate across global application layouts.
- **Canvas Raised (`#0F172A`):** Structural panels, side navigation rails, and analytical page headers.
- **Surface Container Tier 1 (`#131E32`):** Primary card modules, data surfaces, and diagnostic charts.
- **Surface Container Tier 2 (`#18233C`):** Nested inspection blocks, interactive table headers, and focused input fills.
- **Surface Highlight (`#223254`):** Active card borders, hover states, and subtle separators.

### Brand & Intelligence
- **Hydro Radiant Primary (`#00E5FF`):** Signature water intelligence accent for telemetry indicators, primary actions, and primary flow paths.
- **Atmosphere Blue Secondary (`#0EA5E9`):** Supporting data streams, secondary filters, pressure gauges, and inactive track indicators.

### Restrained Semantic Tokens
- **Eco Emerald (`#10B981`):** Water conservation benchmarks, normal operational pressures, optimal recycling rates, and efficiency wins.
- **Amber Warning (`#F59E0B`):** Threshold drifts, scheduled valve maintenance, and flow-rate variances.
- **Coral Crimson (`#F43F5E`):** Pipe bursts, acoustic leak detections, sensor outages, and critical contamination flags. Applied with strict surgical moderation.

### Content & Typography
- **Text Primary (`#F8FAFC`):** Crisp white-tinted slate for headings, primary readouts, and active values.
- **Text Secondary (`#94A3B8`):** Muted cool slate for technical labels, dimensional units, and inactive metadata.
- **Text Tertiary (`#64748B`):** Low-contrast structural annotation, timestamps, and breadcrumb dividers.

## Typography

The type system blends the geometric balance of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for atmospheric editorial headers with the neutral legibility of **Inter** for dense sensor telemetry and operational controls.

- **Numerics & Readouts:** Tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be used for dynamic telemetry values (e.g., liters/sec, PSI, conductivity, cubic meters) to prevent UI jitter during streaming data updates.
- **Label Tracking:** All `label-sm` elements and metadata pills adopt expanded letter-spacing (`0.04em`) to maintain sharp readability against darker backgrounds.
- **Hierarchy Separation:** Large headlines set the tone for environmental reporting, while telemetry cards preserve balanced proportions through structured titles (`title-md`) paired with crisp small body units (`body-sm`).

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column fluid responsive grid anchored by clean structural rules. Space is treated as a strategic asset to convey precision and system health, avoiding cramped operational boards.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop displays leverage a 12-column layout with `2rem` gutters and minimum `3rem` canvas margins. Tablet uses an 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters. Mobile switches to 4 columns with `1rem` margins and gutters.
- **Spatial Rhythm:**
  - Component internals strictly use `space-md` (`1rem`) to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) padding, giving card contents comfortable perimeter separation.
  - Inter-card structural gaps strictly use `space-lg` to `space-xl` to enforce module grouping and prevent visual bleeding.
- **Reflow Logic:** Data visualization dashboards collapse gracefully from multi-column metrics (4x1 on desktop) to stacked analytical pairs (2x2 on tablet) and continuous cards (1x1 on mobile), preserving telemetry chart aspect ratios.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through tonal stratification, subtle soft glass panels, and razor-sharp 1px bounding lines rather than heavy drop shadows.

- **The Slate Strata Model:**
  - *Base Level (Elevation 0):* Deepest background `#0B111E`.
  - *Raised Paneling (Elevation 1):* `#0F172A` with a 1px border of `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.35)`.
  - *Data Containers & Cards (Elevation 2):* `#131E32` with a 1px border of `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.45)`.
  - *Floating Dialogs & Overlays (Elevation 3):* `#18233C` combined with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and subtle cyan edge tint `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Soft Ambient Glow:** When highlighting a selected node or critical leak status, an ultra-diffused shadow is applied: `0 0 32px -8px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.15)` for normal active states, or `0 0 32px -8px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.2)` for anomaly alerts.
- **Glass Accents:** Secondary tags, sensor counters, and filter capsules utilize semi-transparent slates (`rgba(24, 35, 60, 0.65)`) with a `12px` backdrop blur to blend smoothly over hydro-geographic maps and stream graphs.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry:
- Default elements (buttons, inputs, status cards) utilize `0.5rem` (8px) radius.
- Structural cards and floating panels use `1rem` (16px) radius.
- System metrics capsules, filters, and modal containment shells utilize `1.5rem` (24px) radius.
- Micro tags, status indicators, and operational switches adopt full pill geometry (`9999px`) to distinguish interactive controls from content containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#00E5FF` fill with dark slate text (`#0B111E`), font-weight 600, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`, rounded `0.5rem`. Hover transitions to a soft radiant lift with `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.3)`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `rgba(19, 30, 50, 0.6)`, 1px border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.25)`, text `#F8FAFC`. On hover: border `#00E5FF`, text `#00E5FF`, backdrop blur `8px`.
- **Destructive / Anomaly Action:** Translucent coral `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.12)` with solid border `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.4)`, text `#F43F5E`. Hover deepens background to `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.2)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Configuration:** Height `24px` to `28px`, padding `0 12px`, rounded `9999px`.
- **Telemetry State Chips:**
  - *Healthy Flow:* `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)` fill, `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)` border, `#10B981` text, with a pulsing 6px emerald indicator dot.
  - *System Anomaly:* `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.1)` fill, `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.3)` border, `#F43F5E` text.
  - *Neutral Filter:* `rgba(24, 35, 60, 0.7)` fill, `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.5)` border, `#94A3B8` text.

### Cards & Module Containers
- Built on `#131E32` with 1px border `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.4)`.
- Internal padding: `space-lg` (`1.5rem`).
- Header zones feature prominent metric readouts paired with subtle micro-trend indicators (e.g., `+2.4% vs baseline`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#0F172A`, 1px border `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.5)`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#64748B`.
- Rounded `0.5rem`, height `44px`, padding `0 1rem`.
- Focus state triggers a clean 1px border highlight of `#00E5FF` with a zero-offset inner glow `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #00E5FF`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `18px`, rounded `4px` (checkbox) or `9999px` (radio).
- Default: Border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.4)` on `#0F172A`.
- Checked: Fill `#00E5FF`, checkmark icon colored `#0B111E`.

### Hydraulic Flow Sliders & Gauge Bars
- Track height `6px`, background `#18233C`, border-radius `9999px`.
- Active fill: Horizontal gradient from `#0EA5E9` to `#00E5FF`.
- Thumb: `18px` circle, `#F8FAFC` fill, bordered with `2px solid #00E5FF`, elevation shadow `0 2px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.

### Anomaly Alert Banners
- Architectural banner docked to the card or page head.
- Background: Gradient from `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.08)` to `rgba(19, 30, 50, 0.4)`, bordered on the left with a solid `3px` stripe of `#F43F5E`.
- Text styled with `body-sm` in `#F8FAFC`, metadata in `#F43F5E`.
